## Top issues

1. **israel-extends-law-east-jerusalem-1967 (placeholder) now has real sources, all read.** They confirm the dates as 27-28 June 1967.
   - **UN S/8146 = A/6793** (12 Sep 1967), paras 13 and 38-40:
     - 27 June: the Law and Administration Ordinance (Amendment No. 11) and the Municipal Corporations Ordinance (Amendment No. 7).
     - 28 June: the government orders and the extension of the municipal boundary. The city area grew by more than 60 km², to over 100 km².
     - Annex II holds a protest by Jerusalem Arab notables against the "annexation".
   - **Israeli Supreme Court, Ben-Dov v Minister of Religious Affairs** (27 Mar 1968): calls the measures "a clear act of sovereignty".
   - **JTA, 12 Jul 1967:** Foreign Minister Eban says "annexation" is "out of place".
   - **UNGA 2253** (4 Jul 1967, 99-0-20) and **UNSC 252** (21 May 1968, 13-0-2): both call the measures "invalid".
   - The current perspective "Justice Kahan 1970, 'was annexed'" could not be found in any source. Replace it with the Ben-Dov wording.
   - The pin sits in West Jerusalem and should move to the Old City.
2. **Figures corrected from primary sources:**
   - **Khan Yunis and Rafah:** the UNRWA report A/3212/Add.1 lists 275 named dead at Khan Yunis (3 Nov) and 111 at Rafah camp (12 Nov). It also records both sides: the Israeli claim of resistance and the refugees' denial.
   - **Kafr Qasim:** 48 killed, or 49 counting an unborn child. The eight men convicted in 1958 were found guilty of killing 43 (JTA 1958).
   - **UNRWA:** UN sources give about 750,000 refugees (not 700,000) and a start of operations on 1 May 1950. FRUS gives the founding vote as 47-0-6.
3. **lydda-ramle-expulsions-1948:**
   - The summary's "accounts differ on whether the departure was ordered" understates the evidence. An IDF expulsion order signed by Rabin exists (12 Jul 1948, 13:30; Morris 2004 p. 429).
   - The real dispute is about Ben-Gurion's role and intent, plus Allon's 1979 denial.
   - The Kadish and Sela perspective has not been confirmed in what was read.
4. **Perspectives that rest on sources nobody read:**
   - **end-of-military-government-1966:** the "PalQuest 1968" claim comes from a search snippet only.
   - **land-day-1976:** "three of the dead were women" is doubtful; search results suggest only one woman among the six dead.
   - **law-of-return-1950:** "unanimously" rests on Wikipedia alone.
5. **absentees-property-law-1950:** the day is confirmed from the law text. It passed on 14 March 1950, so the date can move to day precision.

Checks the brief asked for:
- **samu-raid-1966:** 13 Nov 1966 is confirmed by the text of UNSC 228 and by history.state.gov.
